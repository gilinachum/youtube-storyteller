# Webinar / Remote Session Planning

## The Remote Challenge

Remote audiences have:
- **Lower attention span** — distractions everywhere (email, Slack, kids, phone)
- **No social pressure** — easy to leave silently or zone out
- **Reduced feedback** — speaker can't see faces, read the room
- **Technical barriers** — audio issues, bandwidth, platform unfamiliarity

Your enemy is the **mute button + second monitor**. Design to compete with email.

## Planning Process

1. **Define format** — webinar (one-way) vs. meeting (interactive)?
2. **Set duration** — 30-45 min max (60 min only if highly interactive)
3. **Design for engagement** — interaction every 5-7 minutes
4. **Plan the tech** — platform, backup, recording
5. **Create the run-of-show** — minute-by-minute plan with co-host roles

## Session Structure

### Pre-Session (5-10 min before)
- Open room early with a welcome slide + music or intro video
- Display logistics: agenda, how to ask questions, will it be recorded?
- Moderator greets early joiners in chat ("Welcome! Where are you joining from?")

### Opening (3-5 min)
- Start ON TIME (respect remote schedules — they blocked a calendar slot)
- Brief intro (30 sec max — remote audiences are impatient)
- Set expectations: duration, interaction style, recording notice
- First engagement within 2 minutes: poll, chat question, or hand raise
- "Type in chat: what's your #1 challenge with X?"

### Body (70-80% of time)
- **Chunked into 7-10 min segments** — each with:
  - A key point or concept
  - A visual (demo, diagram, example)
  - An interaction (poll, chat prompt, or question)
- **Vary the medium** every few minutes:
  - Slides → demo → back to slides → video clip → whiteboard
- **Chat-driven content** — reference chat responses, read names
- **Energy variation** — speed up/slow down, change vocal register

### Q&A (10-15 min)
- Use a Q&A tool (Slido, Zoom Q&A) so questions queue up
- Upvoting helps prioritize
- Read the question before answering (gives remote context)
- "Great question from [name]..." — makes it personal
- Have 2-3 pre-prepared questions as backup (plant if needed)

### Closing (2-3 min)
- 3-bullet recap (repeat key takeaways)
- Clear next steps / CTA
- Resources link (drop in chat)
- "Thank you" + recording availability timeline
- Feedback survey link in chat

## Best Practices

### Engagement Tactics (use 3+ per session)
- **Polls** — quick 2-4 option polls at natural breakpoints
- **Chat storms** — "everyone type your answer in 3, 2, 1... GO"
- **Breakout rooms** — 5-min pair discussions (works for < 50 people)
- **Live demos** — real-time building keeps attention
- **Audience names** — call out chat contributors by name
- **Surprise reveals** — "I'm about to show you something most people don't know..."
- **Cold calls** (carefully) — "Sarah, I see you work at X — does this resonate?"

### Technical Delivery
- **Camera ON** — your face creates connection (much higher engagement than slides-only)
- **Eye contact with camera** — look at the lens, not the screen
- **Good lighting** — face lit from front, not backlit
- **Clean background** — virtual or physical, no distractions
- **Wired internet** — don't risk wifi drops
- **Close all notifications** — Slack, email, system alerts
- **Share specific window**, not full screen (prevents notification leaks)
- **Increase font sizes** — 24pt minimum for screen-shared code/terminal

### Voice & Energy
- Speak **15% louder and 20% more energetically** than you would in person
- Vary your pace — slow down for key points, speed up for transitions
- Use **silence** intentionally (2 seconds feels dramatic on Zoom)
- Stand up while presenting if possible — changes vocal energy
- Smile — it's audible in your voice

### Attendee Experience
- Send reminder email 1 day before + 1 hour before
- Include clear join instructions (not everyone knows Zoom/Chime)
- Offer dial-in as backup for poor internet
- Record and share — assume 30-50% will watch the recording instead
- Share slides as PDF after (or before, for followers)

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ No interaction for the first 15+ minutes (you've lost them)
- ❌ Slides with too much text (unreadable at screen-share resolution)
- ❌ Ignoring the chat (messages pile up, attendees feel invisible)
- ❌ Camera off (just a voice over slides = podcast, not a session)
- ❌ Going over time (remote attendees have hard stops — back-to-back meetings)
- ❌ "Can everyone hear me?" as the opening (boring, kills energy)
- ❌ Not recording (50% of registrants watch later)
- ❌ Sharing full desktop (notifications, private tabs visible)
- ❌ No co-host/moderator (speaker can't monitor chat AND present)

## Checklist

### 1 Week Before
- [ ] Platform selected and tested (Zoom webinar vs. meeting vs. Chime)
- [ ] Registration/invite sent with clear join instructions
- [ ] Co-host/moderator assigned
- [ ] Polls created in the platform
- [ ] Q&A tool configured
- [ ] Recording settings verified
- [ ] Calendar hold sent (with 5-min buffer before/after)

### Day Before
- [ ] Tech rehearsal with co-host (test screen share, polls, breakout rooms)
- [ ] Slides finalized (check font size at screen-share resolution)
- [ ] Demo environment prepped (tabs open, clean desktop)
- [ ] Backup plan: if internet dies, who takes over?
- [ ] Reminder email sent

### 30 Min Before
- [ ] Close all apps except presentation tools
- [ ] Do Not Disturb mode ON
- [ ] Camera, mic, lighting tested
- [ ] Water within reach
- [ ] Notes/timer visible (but not on shared screen)
- [ ] Welcome slide displayed
- [ ] Chat monitored by moderator

### After
- [ ] Recording processed and shared (< 24h)
- [ ] Follow-up email with: recording link, slides, resources, feedback survey
- [ ] Review Q&A — answer anything missed
- [ ] Check attendance/engagement data for future improvement

## Duration Guidelines

| Format | Ideal Duration | Max Duration |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| Lunch & Learn | 25-30 min | 45 min |
| Webinar (external) | 45 min | 60 min |
| Internal tech talk | 30-45 min | 60 min |
| Training session | 90 min (with break) | 2 hours |
| Deep dive / multi-part | Split into 45-min segments | N/A |

Rule of thumb: if it's > 45 min, you need either a break or VERY high interaction.
