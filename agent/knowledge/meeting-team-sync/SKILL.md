# Team Sync Meeting Planning

## Purpose

A team sync aligns a team around **status, blockers, decisions, and priorities** for the coming period. Done well it creates clarity and momentum. Done poorly it's a meeting that could have been an email.

**Core question every team sync should answer:** *"What does everyone need to know to do their best work this week?"*

## When to Use This Format

- Weekly or bi-weekly team meetings
- Sprint planning or sprint reviews (adapt timing accordingly)
- Cross-functional alignment meetings where multiple sub-teams converge

## Optimal Cadence & Duration

| Team Size | Cadence | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 3-6 people | Weekly | 30 min |
| 7-12 people | Weekly | 45 min |
| 13-20 people | Weekly | 60 min |
| > 20 people | Consider splitting by sub-team | — |

Teams larger than 12 should split into sub-team syncs + a leads sync.

## Agenda Template

### 1. Opening / Quick Wins (3-5 min)
- Celebrate a win from last week — big or small
- Acknowledge something the team should know (new member, company news)
- Sets a positive, energized tone

### 2. Status Round (10-15 min)
Each person (or work stream) answers **3 questions only**:
- ✅ What did I complete since last sync?
- 🔄 What am I working on now?
- 🚫 What's blocking me?

**Strict rule:** No deep-diving on solutions here. Blockers get **parked** and addressed after the meeting or in a focused break-out.

Keep a "Parking Lot" section in your agenda doc for items that need follow-up.

### 3. Shared Priorities (5-10 min)
- What's the team's #1 priority this week?
- Any deadline shifts or priority changes?
- Resource conflicts to surface?

### 4. Decisions & Announcements (5 min)
- Any decisions made since last sync that the team needs to know
- Upcoming milestones or deadlines
- Anything coming from leadership that's relevant

### 5. Open Floor (5 min)
- Anyone else have something to add?
- Keeps the door open for things not captured in the agenda

### 6. Close (2 min)
- Review action items from today
- Confirm next sync date/time

## Async-First Prep

Distribute a shared agenda doc before the meeting. Ask team members to pre-fill their status updates asynchronously.

**Benefits:**
- Meeting time shrinks (no one recites a wall of updates)
- Introverts get a voice
- Agenda reveals blockers before the call

**Pre-meeting doc template:**
```
Team Sync — [Date]

🏆 Win of the Week:
[Team lead fills in]

Status Updates (fill before the meeting):

[Name]:
  ✅ Done: 
  🔄 In Progress: 
  🚫 Blocked: 

Parking Lot / Topics to Discuss:
- 

Action Items from Last Week:
- [ ] 
```

## Facilitation Tips

### Starting on Time
- Start exactly on time even if 2 people are missing — latecomers catch up from notes
- This trains the team that the meeting respects everyone's time

### Timekeeping
- Assign a timekeeper to gently flag when a topic is running over
- Use a visible timer (share screen with a stopwatch)

### Staying on Topic
- "Great point — let's put that in the parking lot and cover it after"
- "We're getting into solution mode — let's take that offline with [name]"

### Engagement
- Call on quiet team members by name (gently): "Dan, anything from your end this week?"
- Rotate who leads the sync — builds ownership and develops facilitation skills
- Mix in a quick ice-breaker question monthly to build team cohesion

## Parking Lot Rules

Items parked during the sync must be addressed within 24 hours:
- Either resolved async (Slack/email)
- Or scheduled as a focused follow-up meeting

Never let the parking lot become a graveyard of forgotten topics.

## Common Anti-Patterns

| Problem | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Status theater | Everyone recites long updates nobody uses | Require async pre-fill; use sync for blockers only |
| No decisions made | Team is "aligned" but nothing moves | Add "Decision needed" section explicitly |
| Meeting inflation | Keeps growing to 90 min | Time-cap each section; cut items ruthlessly |
| Same person dominates | One voice, others tune out | Rotating facilitator; explicit round-robin |
| No follow-through | Action items never reviewed | Open every sync by reviewing last week's items |
| Hybrid neglect | Remote people can't participate | Camera-on culture; repeat room questions aloud |

## Metrics to Track

A healthy team sync improves over time. Watch:
- **Meeting duration trend** — should decrease as team gets aligned async
- **Parking lot size** — large parking lot = team has real unresolved issues
- **Action item completion rate** — low rate = commitments aren't being honored
- **Attendance** — consistent absences = meeting perceived as low value

## Hebrew / Israeli Context

- Israelis tend toward direct, informal team communication — "וואלה מה קורה?" check-ins are common
- Debate and pushback in team settings are normal and healthy — don't mistake directness for conflict
- "חד פעמי" (one-off) side conversations are common after syncs — leave 5 min buffer
- WhatsApp groups often parallel formal syncs — consider what belongs where
